What we do

Roofing services in Penzance and West Cornwall

From a single slipped slate to a full re-roof, we take on the whole job ourselves and leave it tidy. If you're not sure what your roof needs, give us a call and we'll take a look.

Slate and tile roofing

Full re-roofs and new roofs in natural slate, fibre cement slate or tile. We fit slate on the hook system as well as traditional nailed, so it stays put in a Cornish gale.

Roof repairs

Slipped or broken slates, leaks, rotten battens, failed ridges and valleys. We find the actual cause, fix it, and tell you honestly if the roof has more life left in it.

Storm damage

Quick response after bad weather to make your roof safe and watertight, then a proper repair once the wind drops. Photos and a written quote for your insurer if you need one.

Flat roofs and extensions

New and replacement flat roofs on extensions, porches, dormers and garages, with the timber work, insulation and drainage done right underneath.

Leadwork and flashings

Chimney flashings, valleys, aprons and soakers in lead or modern alternatives. Most leaks start at a junction, and that's where we take the most care.

Roof windows, gutters and fascias

Velux and roof window fitting, replacement guttering, fascias and soffits, and chimney work, so the whole roofline is finished in one visit.

About

Based in Penzance, with a strong eye for detail

Mounts Bay Roofing is run by Andreas Calleja-Stayne, a roofer from Penzance. It's a small, local business, which means the person who quotes your job is the person who turns up and does it.

We prioritise what you actually want and need from your roof, explain the options plainly, and don't push work you don't need. Materials are chosen to suit the building and the weather it gets, and we clear up properly at the end of every day.
